# 2026-11-22: USB map of the XTX machine (ASUS ROG STRIX B650E-I, BIOS 4005)

> **Correction (2026-11-23, thread 00, from thread 02's final report):** the `_UPC` explanation below is unproven. The
> same reading of `GUPC` also marks port 1 not connectable, and macOS created that one; for ports 16 and 17 the
> 15-port limit explains it equally well. The map works either way. The USB 3 halves of the rear ports are paired by
> convention and were never seen at SuperSpeed.

## Why the USB audio and Bluetooth were missing

macOS created 13 of the chipset controller's 17 ports (`XH00`, `1022:43f7`). The four it skipped are 11, 12, 16
and 17. All four are internal ports that the firmware's `_UPC` (`GUPC`, bitmask `0x00072C2C`) reports as **not
connectable**. It is not the 15-port limit. Under Linux (capture of 2026-11-19, `linux-pci-20261119-182102.txt`),
port N of the chipset's USB 2 hub is xHCI port N + 5. The gaming mouse confirms this: Linux `1-10`, macOS `POTH` =
xHCI 15. With that:

| Linux (bus 1) | xHCI port | ACPI | Device |
|---|---|---|---|
| port 6 | 11 | `POTD` | ASUS USB audio `0b05:1a5c` |
| port 7 | 12 | `POTE` | ASUS AURA LED controller `0b05:19af` |
| port 10 | 15 | `POTH` | (rear USB 2 Type-A; the mouse on 2026-11-19) |
| port 11 | 16 | `POT8` | MT7922 Bluetooth `0489:e0e2` |
| port 12 | 17 | `POTI` | nothing seen |

USBToolBox detaches the ACPI port objects from every controller it maps (`removeACPIPorts`, kext 1.2.0 source), so
`_UPC` no longer applies and the ports come from the map alone.

## Port walk (macOS, 2026-11-22, owner)

`tools/usbmap/usbwatch.py` (ioreg only; the port's `port` property is the xHCI port number, equal to the ACPI
`_ADR`). Everything unplugged, then the SanDisk Cruzer (USB 2) into every Type-A port and an iPad (USB 2) into every
Type-C port. The case has no front panel, so only the 8 rear ports exist:

Rear panel numbers: the owner's marked photo (board upright, I/O shield seen from behind; Ethernet at the top
left): 1 = the Type-A next to Ethernet, 2-4 = the row of three Type-A below it from right to left, 5 = the
USB 2 port marked BIOS (Flashback), 6 = the other USB 2 port, 7 = the Type-C marked SS 20, 8 = the Type-C marked DP.

| # | Rear port | Controller | USB 2 half (seen) | USB 3 half (paired, unverified) | Firmware `UsbConnector` |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | USB-A 10 Gbps | `XH00` chipset | 10 | 5 | 3 |
| 2 | USB-A 10 Gbps | `XHC1` CPU | 2 | 4 | 3 |
| 3 | USB-A 10 Gbps | `XHC1` | 1 | 3 | 3 |
| 4 | USB-A 10 Gbps | `XHC0` CPU | 2 | 4 | 3 (firmware: 0 on the USB 2 half) |
| 5 | USB-A USB 2 (BIOS Flashback) | `XH00` | 15 | none | 0 (firmware: 3) |
| 6 | USB-A USB 2 | `XHC2` CPU USB 2 | 1 | none | 0 |
| 7 | USB-C 20 Gbps | `XH00` | 6 | 1 | 10 (Type-C with switch) |
| 8 | USB-C 10 Gbps + DisplayPort | `XHC0` | 1 | 3 | 9 (Type-C) |

The two Type-C ports confirm the controller assignment: the 20 Gbps port is the chipset's (only Promontory 21
has Gen 2x2), the DisplayPort one is the CPU's (DP Alt Mode comes from the iGPU). The comments inside
`UTBMap.kext` don't carry these numbers, so the installed EFI stays identical to the repo build.

This matches ASUS's spec (rear: one 20 Gbps Type-C, four 10 Gbps Type-A plus one 10 Gbps Type-C with DisplayPort,
two USB 2.0). The USB 3 halves are paired by the usual layout: the chipset's USB 3 ports 1-5 sit with USB 2 ports
6-10, and on the CPU controllers PRT1 sits with PRT3 and PRT2 with PRT4. No USB 3 device was available, so the USB 3
halves are *(unverified)* until a USB 3 device is plugged in.

## The map (`efi/kexts/UTBMap.kext`, USBToolBox 1.2.0 format)

Matching by the controllers' ACPI names (`IONameMatch`), as the USBToolBox tool does when the names are unique.

- `XH00`: 1, 6 (rear 20 Gbps C), 5, 10 (rear A), 15 (rear USB 2), 11 (audio, internal), 16 (Bluetooth, internal),
  17 (internal, unknown, kept so nothing is lost). 8 ports; `port-count` 17. Left out: 2-4 / 7-9 / 13-14 (the
  front-panel headers, unused) and 12 (the AURA LED controller, which has no macOS software).
- `XHC0`, `XHC1`: all 4 ports each. `XHC2`: its 1 port.

Port numbers above 15 are allowed in a map; the limit is 15 ports per controller.

## Result (boot 2026-11-22 20:00, XTX machine)

- `USBToolBox` 1.2.0 loaded; every controller shows exactly its mapped ports, named from the map (`SS01`, `HS01`, ...).
- `XH00` port 11: `USB Audio` (Realtek USB2.0 Audio, `0b05:1a5c`); `system_profiler SPAudioDataType` lists 6 audio
  devices, one of them the default input and output (2 channels each). Sound out of the jacks: to check by ear.
- `XH00` port 16: `Wireless_Device` `0489:e0e2` (MT7922 Bluetooth) enumerates; no driver attaches yet, and
  Bluetooth settings still show Apple's placeholder `BCM_4350C2` controller (state Off).
- Keyboard, mouse, Xbox controller, SanDisk work; Wi-Fi active; the GPU daemon finished (`done`), 2560x1440, Metal.
